Privacy Policy for EduGrade

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

EduGrade (“we,” “our,” or the “App”) is a school management and educational administration application that provides tools for managing student information, attendance, academic assessments, examination results, report cards, school fees, and staff accounts.

EduGrade provides these tools to schools as a service provider. Each school determines why and how personal information is collected and used through EduGrade and acts as the data controller for the personal information it enters into or manages through the App.

EduGrade is used by school administrators, principals, teachers, and bursars. Schools use the application to process information relating to students, parents, guardians, and staff.

This Privacy Policy explains the information EduGrade processes on behalf of schools, the responsibilities of EduGrade as a service provider, and the responsibilities of each school as the data controller.

By using EduGrade, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect and Process

The information processed through EduGrade depends on the features used by each school and the information entered by authorized school users.

A. Student Information

Schools enter and manage the following student information through EduGrade:

  • Student names and admission numbers.
  • Class, class arm, and academic level.
  • Gender and date of birth, where applicable.
  • Parent or guardian names and contact details.
  • Student photographs, where provided.
  • Other student information required for school administration.

The school determines which student information it collects, the purposes for which it uses that information, and the lawful basis for processing it. EduGrade processes that information to provide the services requested by the school.

B. Academic Information

EduGrade processes academic records entered by authorized school personnel, including:

  • Continuous assessment scores.
  • Test and examination results.
  • Subject grades and grade points.
  • Class positions and academic performance summaries.
  • Teacher comments and principal remarks.
  • Generated student report cards and class broadsheets.

The school is responsible for ensuring that academic information is accurate, lawfully collected, and accessed only by authorized personnel.

C. Attendance Information

EduGrade processes attendance records entered by authorized school personnel, including attendance dates; present, absent, late, and excused status; historical attendance records; and attendance summaries and reports.

Authorized teachers access and edit attendance records for relevant dates according to the permissions configured by the school. The school determines which personnel receive access and remains responsible for the accuracy and lawful use of attendance information.

D. Staff and Account Information

EduGrade processes staff information such as principal, teacher, and bursar names; staff roles and assigned responsibilities; login PINs or other account authentication information; school codes or identifiers used to associate accounts with the correct school; and account settings and access permissions.

PINs control access to staff profiles. Users must keep their PINs confidential. EduGrade protects authentication information according to the security controls implemented in the published application. Schools are responsible for creating and managing staff accounts, assigning appropriate permissions, and removing access when a staff member no longer requires it.

E. Financial and Accounting Information

Where a school uses the bursary features, EduGrade processes school fee records; payment amounts and dates; payment methods, such as cash or bank transfer; receipt numbers and payment references; outstanding balances and fee defaulter records; and school expense records and accounting summaries.

EduGrade is a school administration and record-keeping service. Recording school payments does not make EduGrade a bank, lender, payment processor, or financial institution.

The school is responsible for the accuracy, lawful use, and retention of financial information entered into the App.

F. School Information and Documents

Schools enter institutional information such as the school name, contact information, logo, academic sessions, terms, grading rules, and other configuration details. EduGrade generates report cards, receipts, broadsheets, and other documents from information entered by authorized school users.

3. How We Use Information

EduGrade processes information on behalf of schools for the following purposes:

  • Providing and operating the school administration features requested by each school.
  • Managing student admissions and school records.
  • Recording and reviewing attendance.
  • Recording continuous assessments and examination scores.
  • Calculating grades and academic performance summaries.
  • Generating report cards and broadsheets.
  • Recording school fee payments and expenses.
  • Managing principal, teacher, and bursar accounts.
  • Restricting access to role-specific features.
  • Synchronizing school information across authorized devices when cloud synchronization is enabled.
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, securing, and improving the App.
  • Responding to support requests and authorized data deletion requests.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.

Each school determines the purposes and lawful basis for processing the personal information it enters into EduGrade. EduGrade does not use school records for advertising, sale to data brokers, or unrelated commercial purposes.

4. School Accounts and Multi-School Data Separation

EduGrade supports multiple schools, each with its own school identity, staff accounts, and administrative records. Each school must use its designated school code or other configured school identifier when accessing its records.

EduGrade separates school records so that one school's student information, attendance, academic results, and financial records are not accessible to another school through ordinary authorized use of the App.

Within each school, access is controlled by staff roles configured by the school:

  • Principal: School administration and authorized oversight of academic, attendance, staff, and financial records.
  • Teacher: Authorized academic records, assessments, and attendance functions.
  • Bursar: Authorized fee records, receipts, expenses, and accounting functions.

EduGrade implements authentication, database configuration, and backend access rules to enforce school separation and role-based permissions. Schools remain responsible for assigning correct roles, protecting credentials, reviewing access, and promptly reporting suspected unauthorized access.

5. Data Storage and Cloud Synchronization

EduGrade stores information locally on a device and, when cloud synchronization is enabled, synchronizes information with a cloud database so authorized users can access updated school records from different devices.

EduGrade uses Firebase cloud services for synchronization and related cloud functionality. Information is processed or stored using Google Firebase infrastructure according to the deployed configuration.

Cloud synchronization enables authorized principals, teachers, and bursars to access current information without manually transferring records between devices.

Information stored locally remains on a device until it is deleted by the user, removed through the App's supported functions, or cleared through applicable device or application processes.

Uninstalling the App does not delete information already synchronized to a cloud database.

EduGrade acts as the school's service provider for cloud storage and synchronization. The school remains responsible for determining which information is uploaded, who receives access, and how long the information is retained.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

EduGrade does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information is accessible to:

  • Authorized school principals, teachers, and bursars according to permissions assigned by the school.
  • School personnel with legitimate administrative reasons to access relevant records.
  • Cloud infrastructure and other service providers that operate, synchronize, secure, or maintain the App.
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to address legitimate security or legal concerns.

EduGrade discloses and processes school information only to provide the services, follow the school's documented instructions, maintain and secure the App, or comply with legal obligations.

Each school is responsible for ensuring that information is collected, entered, used, and accessed only for lawful school purposes and by appropriately authorized personnel. The school is also responsible for responding to requests from students, parents, guardians, staff, and other data subjects concerning information controlled by the school.

Google Firebase and other service providers used by EduGrade process information under their applicable terms and privacy documentation.

7. Children's Privacy and Student Records

EduGrade processes information about school-age students, including children under 13, when a school enters student records into the App.

The school is the data controller for student information and is responsible for obtaining consent, authorization, or another lawful basis required to collect and process student information; providing required notices to students, parents, and guardians; ensuring that student information is entered and used only for legitimate educational and administrative purposes; preventing public disclosure or unauthorized access to student records; and responding to requests concerning student information.

EduGrade processes student information on the school's instructions and does not independently determine the educational purposes for which the school uses that information.

If a parent, guardian, student, or school representative believes that information has been collected or disclosed improperly, they must contact the relevant school administrator. They may also contact EduGrade using the support details in this policy where the concern relates to EduGrade's operation of the service.

8. Data Security

EduGrade protects student, staff, academic, and financial information using role-based access restrictions, account authentication, and local and cloud security measures implemented in the published application.

Cloud communications use encrypted connections, including HTTPS/TLS, where supported by the deployed services.

No application, storage system, or electronic transmission method eliminates every security risk.

EduGrade is responsible for implementing and maintaining security controls for the App and the services it operates. Schools are responsible for keeping PINs and device credentials confidential; avoiding the use of unlocked devices by unauthorized persons; using appropriate device security, including a screen lock; assigning access only to appropriate staff members; reviewing staff access and removing unnecessary permissions; and reporting suspected account compromise or unauthorized access to EduGrade promptly.

The security of school information also depends on the school's account management, device security, staff practices, and configuration of the App.

9. Data Retention

EduGrade retains information for as long as necessary to provide the services, maintain school records, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and meet legitimate operational and security requirements.

Each school determines the retention periods for student, attendance, academic, staff, and financial records and remains responsible for complying with applicable retention obligations.

When a school instructs EduGrade to delete information and no legal or operational requirement requires continued retention, EduGrade deletes the information using the available deletion mechanisms.

Deleting information from a device does not necessarily remove corresponding cloud copies, backups, exported documents, or records held separately by the school.

10. Data Deletion and User Requests

Each school manages the personal information it controls and responds to legitimate requests concerning that information.

Authorized school administrators can delete student records, staff records, and other information through the App's available deletion functions.

For information that cannot be deleted directly in the App, users must contact the relevant school administrator. EduGrade assists the school with deletion requests relating to information processed through the service.

Deletion requests must identify the relevant school and the information concerned without unnecessarily disclosing sensitive personal information.

Some records must be retained to comply with applicable laws or legitimate school record-keeping obligations. Where deletion is not possible, the school is responsible for explaining the applicable reason.

Uninstalling EduGrade does not delete cloud-stored information.

11. Permissions and Device Features

EduGrade requests access to device features needed for functions such as selecting student photographs, choosing a school logo, generating documents, or saving and sharing reports.

Permissions requested by the published application are limited to functions required for its operation. The exact permissions and data accessed depend on the installed version and the Android APIs used by that version.

Users can review and manage applicable Android permissions through their device settings.

12. Third-Party Services

EduGrade uses third-party infrastructure and services to provide cloud synchronization, database storage, application security, crash reporting, application distribution, and related functions.

EduGrade uses Firebase services where configured in the published application. Firebase processes technical information and application data according to its configuration and applicable privacy terms.

Users can review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy and security information at Firebase Privacy and Security.

EduGrade remains responsible for selecting and managing service providers used to operate the service. Each school remains responsible for ensuring that its use of those services complies with applicable law and its obligations as data controller.

13. International Data Processing

Cloud services used by EduGrade process or store information in locations outside the country where a school or user is located.

EduGrade applies safeguards required for international data transfers under applicable data protection laws.

Schools are responsible for considering international transfer requirements that apply to the personal information they enter into EduGrade and for providing any required notices or obtaining any required authorizations.
