OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

School Result Generator & Portal

Package Name: com.aistudio.schoolresults.vqpznx • Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of School Result Generator & Portal ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") regarding the collection, processing, and protection of information processed through our educational mobile application.

The Application is engineered for educational institutions, primary and secondary school principals, teachers, and bursars to record Continuous Assessment (CA) scores, compute examination marks, generate WAEC-compliant term broadsheets, issue terminal report cards, and manage school fee records.

We are committed to the highest standards of data security, child privacy protection, and compliance with the Google Play Developer Policies, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

2. Educational Data Collection & Purpose

Zero-Commercialization Promise: Student personal data, grades, and school finances are NEVER sold, rented, leased, or monetized for advertising purposes.

The Application processes educational records entered by authorized school staff for legitimate academic operations:

A. Student Biodata & Academic Scores

  • Student Identity: Full name, admission number, gender, date of birth, class arm (e.g. JSS 1A, SS 2 Science).
  • Academic Performance: Continuous assessment scores (CA1, CA2, assignments), terminal examination marks, grade points, overall percentage, and class ranking.
  • Attendance Records: Daily morning roll call and percentage attendance for the term.
  • Student Photos: Selected by administrators using the Android system photo picker to produce photographic report cards.

B. Guardian & Parent Details

  • Parent Phone Numbers: Used solely to allow staff to send terminal results and fee receipts via standard device messaging (SMS and WhatsApp).

C. School Financial Records

  • Bursary Data: Tuition fee structures, recorded fee payments, outstanding balances, and school operational expenses.

D. Staff Credentials

  • User Accounts: Staff usernames, roles (Principal, Teacher, Bursar, Developer), and secure authentication PINs to enforce department-level access control.

3. Local Device Storage Architecture

All student biodata, assessment marks, financial ledgers, and institutional configurations are stored locally and privately on the school's authorized Android device using an encrypted local SQLite database (Android Room Persistence Library).

  • Database files are isolated within the application's secure private sandbox.
  • No unapproved third parties have access to student academic records.
  • School administrators maintain complete sovereignty over their data.

4. Permissions Disclosed

The Application requests only the minimal permissions necessary to operate:

  • INTERNET: Enables optional AI-assisted teacher remarks and launching external WhatsApp/SMS sharing intents.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used on Android 13+ to provide reminders regarding result compilation deadlines.
  • Zero-Permission Media Picker: Utilizes Android's zero-permission Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia) for school logos and student portraits without needing broad file storage permissions.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Minor Protection)

Because the Application records academic grades for students under 18:

  • The Application is operated strictly by school administrators acting in loco parentis.
  • No behavioral profiling, ad-tracking, or tracking SDKs are embedded in the software.
  • Parents and legal guardians may review, correct, or request the deletion of their child's records through their school administration.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

  • Individual Records: Administrators can modify or permanently delete any student, class, score, or financial record directly within the app.
  • Complete Database Wipe: Administrators can clear or reset all data via the School Settings menu.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application completely erases all local database records and cached data from the device.

7. Developer Console Account Protection

To safeguard the application's integrity, institutional licensing, and prevent system lockout, the Developer Console account is permanently protected from deletion by school staff accounts.
